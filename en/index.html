IDB credit line · Regional Development Funds

US$ 500 million for infrastructure in Brazil’s North, Northeast and Central-West

The Inter-American Development Bank (IDB) will capitalize three Brazilian regional development funds, FDA, FDNE and FDCO, so they can lend long-term, local-currency (Brazilian real) credit to private companies and concessionaires. The focus is transport, logistics, water and sanitation projects, fully private or structured as public-private partnerships (PPPs), that do not reach financial close with market credit today.

The IDB expects the program to benefit about 2 million people, including 1.2 million with expanded access to water and sewerage.

Target projects

What the credit line finances

Mid-sized projects, from concessionaires with regulated contracts or fully private sponsors. They are economically viable projects that need long tenors and lower funding costs to reach financial close.

Logistics and integration

Railways, highways, logistics terminals and airport terminals.

River and maritime transport

Waterways, ports and terminals; construction, renewal and retrofit of vessels.

Urban and electric mobility

Transport decarbonization, electrification and fleet renewal.

Water and sanitation

Concessions aimed at universal service coverage.

Terms

General financing terms

The same for all three funds, set by Brazil’s National Monetary Council (CMN) in Resolution No. 4,960/2021.

Fund shareup to 60% of infrastructure investment in priority areas (50% elsewhere). Water supply and sanitation can reach 80%.
Sponsor equityat least 20% of total investment. The remainder may come from other sources.
Tenorup to 20 years for infrastructure, including the grace period. Semiannual principal and interest payments.
Grace periodup to 1 year after operations begin, with capitalized interest. Construction counts within the grace period: a project with 3 years of construction may go up to 4 years without amortizing.
Interest rateIPCA + 4.4% to 11.8% p.a. depending on region, sector and location. The real component is the fixed-rate TLP, the benchmark long-term rate published monthly in Brazil, adjusted by a regional coefficient and by project type, and locked in the month the contract is signed.
Collateral and riskSet by the operating bank, which bears 100% of the credit risk.

    Disclaimers

    Simulation terms

    For information only. It does not replace the assessment of the regional development agencies or the operating banks.

    • Results are a simulation and do not constitute a financing offer. Payment amounts are approximate.
    • The rate follows current legislation and the parameters shown in the simulator. Each contract’s rate is fixed in the month the contract is signed, and the outstanding balance is indexed to IPCA inflation.
    • Tenor, grace period and number of installments depend on repayment capacity and on credit approval by the operating bank.
    • Rates, tenors and other terms may be changed by Brazil’s National Monetary Council and by the councils of the regional development agencies.
    • Contracting depends on the availability of fund resources and on compliance with regulatory requirements.
    • Eligibility, project type and the share limit are set by the regional development agency. The classification of FDA municipalities follows Sudam’s simulator; for FDNE and FDCO, spatial priority is a suggestion to be confirmed.
    • Implementation of the IDB credit line depends on the completion of approvals in Brazil.
    • This English version is provided for convenience. In case of any discrepancy, the Portuguese version and the official Brazilian regulations prevail.
